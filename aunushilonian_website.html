Who We Are

More Than a School Connection

Aunushilonian brings together generations of the Aunushilon Academy family — students, alumni and teachers — to stay connected, learn from one another and create meaningful opportunities.

Our Vision

To build a strong, friendly and inspiring network where Aunushilonians can share knowledge, celebrate achievements, support students, preserve memories and contribute to the future of the academy community.

Our Community

What Aunushilonians Do

Ideas and activities that keep the Aunushilonian community connected and active.

🎓

Student Support

Encouraging current students through guidance, academic resources, mentorship and experience-sharing.

🤝

Alumni Networking

Connecting former students across different batches, professions and places while keeping the academy bond alive.

🔬

Science & Innovation

Promoting curiosity, scientific thinking, problem-solving and educational competitions.

✍️

Creative Writing

Creating space for poems, stories, essays, science fiction and other original student and alumni work.

🏆

Competitions & Events

Organising educational, intellectual, cultural and community-focused activities for young learners.

🌱

Community Service

Building a culture of responsibility, volunteering, teamwork and positive contribution to the community.

📸

Memories & Heritage

Preserving school memories, stories, achievements and milestones for future generations of Aunushilonians.

💡

Ideas & Leadership

Giving students and alumni opportunities to share ideas, take initiative and develop leadership skills.

🎉

Celebrating Achievements

Recognising academic success, creative achievements, competitions and milestones across the community.

Aunushilonian Initiative

SCIENCE QUEST

Science Quest is an educational quest led by Aunushilonians, designed to encourage scientific curiosity, logical thinking, healthy competition and a love for learning.

Visit Science Quest Page

Explore • Think • Compete

Through science-focused challenges and educational events, Science Quest aims to give students an exciting platform to test knowledge and develop scientific thinking.
